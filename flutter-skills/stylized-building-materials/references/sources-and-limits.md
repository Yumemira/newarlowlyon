# Primary sources and transfer limits

Conclusion: borrow observable principles, not another game's palette or project-specific rules. Reviewed 2026-10-27; summaries below are deliberately narrow.

- [Valve: Dota 2 Character Art Guide](https://help.steampowered.com/en/faqs/view/0688-7692-4D5A-1935): silhouette, value grouping, quiet regions and repeated game-view evaluation support readability. This source concerns characters; using those principles for buildings is an inference. Its body-gradient and hero-identity rules are not architectural requirements here. Search-index text was readable; the direct page fetch exposed little content.
- [Riot: VALORANT Shaders and Gameplay Clarity](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/valorant-shaders-and-gameplay-clarity): rendering choices balance art and game readability; discusses diffuse, specular and ambient response. Its competitive-FPS constraints and rendering implementation are not recommendations for our sprite engine.
- [Columbia GSAPP: Materials, Seth Thompson](https://smorgasbord.cdp.arch.columbia.edu/modules/40-rendering-with-blender/254-materials/): material models include roughness, reflectivity/metalness and transmission beyond base color. Transfer to painted cues is our adaptation. Example PBR values and photorealistic output are not fixed stylized-art presets.

The cue tables, wealth distinctions and critique procedure in this skill are practical design guidance assembled for this workflow, not experimental findings or a claim that a particular game's materials were reconstructed. Final artwork must be evaluated directly. No external code was copied.
