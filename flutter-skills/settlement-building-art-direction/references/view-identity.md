# Building identity across views

Conclusion: a rotation must preserve a building's topology and landmark positions, not merely its colors and decorative theme. This applies to views of one building. For different functions, design distinct architecture; use building-design-diversity rather than enforcing the same house across the set.

Make a local-coordinate identity card: main footprint and long axis, gable ends, roof ridge directions, storeys, entrance wall, window groups, annex attachment wall/offset, chimney along/across ridge, unique markers. Separate observed from assumed hidden elements. Shared world-space light can illuminate different facades after rotation; do not repaint light independently to fake continuity.

When a long gabled building becomes side-on, its gable stays at the END of the ridge. If the original entrance is on that end, a front-facing entrance/gable pasted beside the long side is a structural change, not improved readability. A perpendicular wing is legitimate only if it existed or was deliberately redesigned. Occluded features may disappear; absence behind the roof is not automatically a missing component.

Compare planar directions and attachments first, dimensions second, decoration third. Mark each suspected defect with file/view, visible landmarks, expected local relationship and confidence. Different projected widths across yaw can be physically correct; different canvas sizes or roof/wall area ratios are signals to investigate, not proofs of geometry drift.

Check camera-space near/far order from a persistent model or structural depth guide. Do not keep an annex visible by transferring it to the opposite wall, reduce the building's physical depth to expose its entrance, or normalize each view's width/height independently. Rendered occlusion may change; local identity does not.

Use gables/hips for rigid houses; cross-sections and support ring for organic shells; entrance-to-annex relationships for domes and pots. Count visible windows only after accounting for occlusion and which wall is visible. Do not normalize all races to one architecture or identical door size without the project's scale decision.

Generation packet: project style image + camera/scene reference + projected structural guide + identity card + intended yaw. Require one pilot to preserve these relationships before a batch. If painting repeatedly moves the gable, stretches the body or reattaches the annex, use geometry-controlled paintover or correct manually within authorized scope. Increasing “same style” wording does not fix topology.
