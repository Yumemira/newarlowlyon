# Building design diversity

For an early sketch set, use $building-sketch-guide and stop here: vary dominant masses, proportions and roofs from relevant project references. Do not load the detailed briefs, material studies or functional-architecture references below unless that design stage is requested.

Give each building a design driven by its purpose, site and intended status. Keep the scene's drawing language and camera coherent while allowing substantial architectural invention. An existing house is evidence of rendering style, not a floor plan to reuse everywhere.

## Diagnose the set

Read relevant project art/plans entrypoints and inspect a selection of actual buildings together. Identify repeated footprints, roof topology, entrance placement, annex composition, proportions and material allocation. Distinguish intentional construction traditions from a repeated whole building with substituted props. Do not exaggerate sameness: record successful exceptions too. Independently fitted thumbnails help compare design, but do not prove common world scale or camera.

Read [style-and-identity.md](references/style-and-identity.md) before a new design or alternate-view request. Preserve a building's accepted local identity across its rotations. For different functions, create different architecture as needed. A requirement to match race/style does not establish that every wall/roof must use the same material.

## Design before decorating

For a new functional design read [functional-architecture.md](references/functional-architecture.md): start with activity/adjacency, plan and a section, then express those relationships through masses, openings and roof supports. Keep visual assumptions separate from established game mechanics.

Write a compact design brief with purpose, spatial arrangement, main masses, roof system, structural material, finish/accent hierarchy, wealth/craft level and a distinctive functional feature. State what can be reused from references and what is newly designed. Keep invented gameplay, lore and resource requirements separate from visual concepts.

Generate meaningfully different structural concepts when the task needs exploration; do not count recolors as alternatives. Start from function: a forge can require an open working bay and tall exhaust; a theatre needs stage/audience relationships; a healer can suggest sheltered care rooms and a garden. These are design possibilities, not mandatory gameplay mechanics. Use [materials.md](references/materials.md) for material expression and [concept-review.md](references/concept-review.md) for comparative review.

Change the dominant building organization when it is the source of duplication. Adding a tower, crystal or sign to the same house may leave the underlying repetition intact. Repeated modules such as windows or masonry can support cohesion; repeated complete compositions need an explicit reason. Avoid arbitrary quotas of shapes, colors or ornaments.

## Build and review

Use [brief-and-critique.md](references/brief-and-critique.md) to record an inspectable design brief and revise observation → hypothesis → concrete change → recheck. For surface response and richness at gameplay size, use $stylized-building-materials when available. It complements material placement; it does not determine the footprint.

Use $settlement-building-art-direction for the shared camera/reference packet and $building-spatial-geometry for coherent construction when available. Freeze geometry per building after design selection, then derive its views. Match brushwork, outline, lighting and readable detail density across materials. Read the imagegen skill when actually generating or editing raster art.

Compare a draft with its nearest existing neighbors at gameplay size, in silhouette, subdued/grayscale value and full color as appropriate. Ask what makes its purpose and structure identifiable before relying on tiny signs or inventory props. Grayscale/silhouette views are diagnostic aids, not automatic acceptance rules; color can legitimately communicate role. Material richness must be visible at game scale and preserve a clear focal hierarchy.

If the draft still reads as the same house wearing a costume, revise the footprint, massing, roof or circulation rather than adding more decoration. Keep promising uniqueness that breaks geometry out of the accepted set; correct its construction without reverting automatically to the old house.

Deliver selected concepts or requested artwork, the comparative design rationale and remaining uncertainty. A written concept review is not proof that final sprites pass. Do not overwrite production art or send instructions to other chats without authorization for that work.
