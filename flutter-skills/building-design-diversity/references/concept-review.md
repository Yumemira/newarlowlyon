# Compare concepts before a batch

Conclusion: compare buildings with their nearest visual neighbors and name structural differences. Text descriptors do not prove a successful final image.

A small comparison table can use function, footprint/massing, roof/section, circulation/openings, dominant construction, accent/finish and functional landmark. Add rows for existing neighbors and proposed concepts. Look for repeated combinations, not a mandatory number of changed columns.

Ask whether differences survive removing small props and color accents. If every row is “gable house + right-hand shed”, changing the shed's contents is unlikely to solve the user's concern. Check silhouette, arrangement and occupied/open space, then surface finishes. Shared low-level modules may be desirable and should not be penalized automatically.

Useful optional exercises: a silhouette board for massing, a low-detail blockout for roof/attachment logic, a materials board for finish and a common-scene board for style/scale. Use only those that resolve the current uncertainty. Do not mistake thumbnail fitting for scale matching or an automatic image-similarity score for design judgment.

Example concept directions, not approved project designs:

| Function | Organization | Construction/finish | Distinctive feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forge | long, open working bay | dark stone hearth, iron frame, copper hood | tall exhaust spine |
| Healer | low court with sheltered rooms | pale plaster/stone, carved wood screens | garden and covered gallery |
| Jeweler | compact secure atelier | fine stone portal, bronze grilles, polished dark wood | protected glazed display bay |
| Alchemical school | court with teaching hall and service tower | stone lower masses, copper equipment, glass accents | coherent tower/hall relationship |
| Crystal research hall | central chamber with supporting wings | stone ribs, crystal focal assembly, metal mounts | supported faceted crown |

For each proposed concept state why its space and materials suit the intended visual role, which project traits carry over and which similar existing composition it avoids. Do not infer game bonuses, technology tiers or lore from this design exercise. Final artwork still needs camera, geometry, style and cross-view review.
