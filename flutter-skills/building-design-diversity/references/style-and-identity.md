# Style, tradition and identity

Conclusion: preserve different things at different levels. Conflating them makes every role repeat the same house.

Scene style: shared camera/projection, world scale, lighting convention, brush/edge language, outline weight, contrast and detail density. These can remain stable while silhouettes and materials vary.

Architectural tradition: recurring joinery, arch/cornice vocabulary, construction habits or selected motifs. Treat project-established features as evidence, not a stereotype that forbids all other materials. A decorative family can appear on an open workshop, a courtyard complex and a ceremonial pavilion without copying their whole plans.

Function: activity should shape spatial organization and dominant forms. Doors, covered work areas, public frontage, storage, circulation, roof spans and exhaust can change together. A sign alone gives weak architectural identity.

Wealth/craft: change quality of construction, shaping and finish as well as material cost. Careful stone cutting, detailed joinery, fitted metalwork and a restrained focal inlay can signal richness. Gold on every surface is only one fantasy possibility and may erase hierarchy.

Individual identity: once a design is accepted, its roof topology, entrance/annex relationships, chimney positions and landmarks persist across yaws. Same building, alternate view: preserve. Different building function: invent its architecture within the shared visual language.

Reusing the exact same house can be intentional for a housing variant or an explicitly requested upgrade. Do not generalize that into a universal rule, or require every minor variant to have an unrelated silhouette. Richness and distinctiveness should answer the user's design scope.

For reference packets, label an existing house “rendering/material-language example; footprint and roof composition not prescribed” when that is its role. Explicitly list which motifs may carry over. Retain the functional brief and structural guide as separate references so a generator does not inherit the entire house by default.
